Using the app

Do I need an account?

No. You can browse, search, filter, follow cases, share a case, and report a concern without signing in. Only organizations that post cases have accounts.

How do I get notified about a case, or about new cases?

Open a case and tap Follow for updates to be notified when the organizer posts an update. To hear about new cases, tap the bell icon on the main screen and choose All new cases, Only these categories, or Off. The bookmark icon lists every case you follow.

If you tapped Don't Allow when asked about notifications, turn them back on in iOS Settings under Notifications, then Sadaqah Cases.

How do I stop notifications?

Tap Following on a case to stop following it, set new-case notifications to Off with the bell icon, or turn notifications off for the app in iOS Settings.

Something on a case looks wrong or suspicious.

Open the case, scroll to the bottom, and tap Report a concern. Choose a reason and add a note. You can add a phone number or email if you'd like the organizers to follow up (the form tells you when one is required). Reports go only to the organizers who manage that case. If the form won't open, or you think a case is urgent, email us with the case title.

The app isn't loading, or something is broken.

Check your connection (the app shows a banner when it can't reach the internet), close and reopen the app, and make sure you have the latest version. If it keeps happening, email us with the details above.

Donations

Does the app take donations?

No. Sadaqah Cases never processes donations or handles payment or bank details. The Donate button opens the organization's own donation page in your browser, and what happens there is between you and that organization.

I have a problem with a donation, a receipt, or a refund.

We can't see, change, or refund a donation, because it's made on the organization's own site. Contact the organization directly using the call and email buttons on the case page, or through its donation page. If you believe a case is fraudulent or misleading, please report it as described above.

For organizations

How does my organization post cases?

Tap the shield icon at the top right of the main screen, then Request organizer access. Enter your organization's name, your name, your email address, and a short description. An existing organizer reviews each request, and we email you the decision at the address you gave. Nothing is approved automatically, and we only email you after a decision is made.

I was approved but can't sign in, or I didn't get the email.

Check your spam folder. On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password? to set or reset your password. If you're still stuck, email us from the address you applied with.

How do I delete my organizer account?

Sign in, open Manage Cases, and tap Delete my account at the top of the screen. This removes your sign-in and account details. Cases you posted stay visible until you or another organizer removes them. Email us if you'd like one taken down.

Why can't I add a photo to a case?

Photo uploads are temporarily paused while we add photo moderation. A case that already has a photo still shows it.
